# Alien Field Notes: The Two Day Earth Review

Entry for make.fun competition
`u-swift-raven-16-alien-field-notes-the-two-day-earth-7647b000` ($2 over 10 places).

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) holds a small bank of original
one-line reviews of Earth by an alien tourist, scores each on the judge's
published criteria, and writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`.

Winning line (132 characters):

> Two stars. The locals worship glowing rectangles, queue for bitter bean water, and call it 'morning'. The dogs, though — five stars.

Run: `node main.ts` (or with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set outside the grader).
Deterministic — two runs produce byte-identical output.
